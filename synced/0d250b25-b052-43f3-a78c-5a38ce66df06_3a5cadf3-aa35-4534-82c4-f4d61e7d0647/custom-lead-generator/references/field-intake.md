# Field Intake — targeting businesses with no online footprint

The best orosolu prospects are often **invisible online**: no website, no Google/Yelp/
Maps listing, cash-only, discovered in person. A web scan can't find them, and naming
one produces nothing to verify. That's not the engine failing — it's the highest-need
lead. These come from **the field**: the operator (Jordi) is physically there and
photographs the storefront. The photo + where he's standing IS the recon.

**Two lanes.** Web-scan mode finds the online-visible shops and ranks them by their
gaps. Field-intake mode handles the invisible ones the scan can never surface. Same
outputs (site + Drive entry), different source of truth.

## When to use

The operator provides a **storefront/signage photo** (and, ideally, the address or
cross-streets they're at) for a business that has little or no online presence.
Trigger on "here's a spot I found", a photo of a business door/sign, "build one for
this place", or when a named business can't be verified online.

## Process

1. **Read the photo as primary evidence.** Extract from the signage what's actually
   there: business name, tagline/concept (e.g. "Bufet de Desayunos Mexicanos"), hours,
   and any phone / social handle printed on the door. These are **operator-supplied
   verified** facts — the sign is the source, cite it as "from storefront photo."
2. **Match the brand from the logo.** Recreate the wordmark to match the sign — fonts
   (script + display), colors, festive motifs — so the site looks like *them*. Use the
   storefront photo itself as a real image on the site.
3. **Light web check, no forcing.** Do ONE careful lookup for socials/photos/reviews.
   If found and confirmably the same business, use them. If nothing (truly invisible),
   **stop — do not attach another business's listing** (the classic failure: matching a
   same-name shop at the wrong address). Mark those fields operator-to-confirm.
4. **Get the address from the operator.** For an invisible business this is the one
   fact only they have — they're standing there. Ask for it plainly; don't guess.
5. **Build presence-first, social-forward.** No footprint means the gap isn't
   ordering — it's *existing online at all*. Lead with: their branded page, follow
   buttons + handles (placeholders to confirm), a tag-us hashtag, working share
   (native share / copy-link / WhatsApp), and photo **upload slots** for their food
   (never reuse another shop's photos). Then the orosolu pitch: presence + socials +
   take cards.
6. **Score honestly.** Invisible = high *need* but demand can't be auto-scored (no
   reviews). Tier it **"Field prospect"** (or A on need with demand UV), not a numeric
   score pretending to verified demand.

## Output

Same as any run: the branded site in the deploy package, and a Drive entry — but the
row reads "invisible online," address/phone/socials **por confirmar**, and the pitch
is presence-first. Everything not on the sign is operator-to-confirm, never invented.

## Why this is the edge

Owner.com-style scanning competes for the shops everyone can already find. Field
intake captures the ones nobody can find — the operators who most need to exist online
and take cards. Jordi in the field + this mode = leads no competitor's scan produces.

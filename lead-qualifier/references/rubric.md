# Means & Qualification Rubric

The job: separate **showed interest / on a list** from **actually able to do the deal**. Judge each lead
on TWO axes — (A) is it really them, and (B) do they have the means/authority — using public evidence.

## A. Identity confidence (gate — must pass before any tier above UNVERIFIED)
Confirm the person on the list is the person you found. Require agreement on at least two of:
- name + company/firm
- name + city/metro
- name + line of work (real estate / investing / the relevant field)
- a self-consistent profile (their site names them; their LinkedIn lists the company; etc.)

**Namesake test:** if the strongest hit is a same-name person at a different company / city / industry,
that is NOT your lead. Verdict `UNVERIFIED — identity unconfirmed`. Do not attach their links.

## B. Means & authority signals (drive the tier)
### Strong means signals (any one → HIGH, once identity is verified)
- **Owns or runs a fund / firm** — founder, principal, GP, managing partner, CEO/CIO of an investment or
  operating company (verified on their own site or LinkedIn company page).
- **Documented deals / portfolio** — press, case studies, "$X AUM", acquisitions, a track record.
- **Accredited-investor / capital signals** — runs a syndication, raises capital, family office, fund
  filings, "accredited" language, large transactions.
- **Substantial real business footprint** — real company website + verified profiles + team, not a shell.
- **Authority to decide** — title implies they can commit capital (owner, partner, principal, C-suite).

### Soft signals (→ MEDIUM)
- Works in the space but as staff / junior, or role/capital unclear.
- Real professional profile, but no evidence of capital or decision authority.
- Small/early operator with presence but no track record yet.

### Low-value signals (→ LOW, even if identity is verified)
- Employee/individual contributor with no ownership or capital role.
- Aspirant / student / "learning to invest" / content-follower with no firm or deals.
- Only a personal social account, no business/professional footprint.
- Service provider adjacent to the space but not a principal (e.g. a junior agent, an intern).

## Tier assignment
| Tier | Condition |
|---|---|
| **HIGH** | Identity verified **and** ≥1 strong means signal |
| **MEDIUM** | Identity verified **and** only soft signals |
| **LOW** | Identity verified **and** no means signals |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Identity not confirmed / only namesakes |

## Evidence requirement
Every non-UNVERIFIED verdict must cite what you actually read: the URL(s) and the specific fact (title,
firm, deal, AUM). "HIGH — runs $200M fund per caliberco.com/about + linkedin.com/company/caliber" is a
verdict; "HIGH — seems legit" is not. If the only support is an aggregator/directory, downgrade a notch
and note it needs corroboration.

## Adapting to a non-investor list
The default rubric is tuned for investor / JV / capital-partner leads. For a different field, keep the
two-axis structure and swap section B's "means" for that field's real qualifier (a B2B buyer's budget +
authority; a hiring lead's headcount + role; etc.). Identity confidence (A) and the evidence requirement
never change.
